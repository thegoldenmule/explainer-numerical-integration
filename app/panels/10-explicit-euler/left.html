Euler, step by step, to the disk

Euler’s method is follow-the-tangent: at the current point, take the slope the equation gives, walk along it for one step h, land, and repeat. Each step is drawn one at a time here, and the polyline is the red curve from panel 3.

Apply it to the modal equation, x′=λx:

xi+1=xi+hλxi=(1+hλ)xi

Then we find the characteristic polynomial p(z)=0 of this linear difference equation by setting xi+1−(1+hλ)xi=0 and replacing xi+j with zj:

p(z)=z−(1+hλ)=0

The only root is z=1+hλ, and the step multiplies the error by it. By hand, for the current eigenvalue: |1+0.033(−0.05+10.00i)|=1.0525.

Why a disk: |1+hλ|≤1 is |λ−(−1/h)|≤1/h, every λ within 1/h of the point −1/h. The hand computation is one point of that disk.

Step the tangent walk forward one step at a time, then change h and redo the number.
