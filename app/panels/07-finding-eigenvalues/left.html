Quadratics and complex numbers

The characteristic polynomial of the spring is a quadratic in λ, and you actually know how to find its roots. It’s the one equation you remember from high school:

λ= −0.1±0.12−4·1·100 2·1

Everything hangs on the part under the root, the discriminant c2−4mk=−399.99. Positive, and there are two real roots on the number line. Zero, and they coincide. Negative, and the root of a negative number is not on the line at all.

That is where i=−1 comes in. A complex number a+bi is a point on a plane: a along the real axis, b along the imaginary one. Its modulus |λ|=a2+b2 is its distance from the origin. For the current spring, λ=−0.05±10.00i, with modulus 10.00.
