Geometric growth

One step: error in, error out. If a step multiplies the error by z, then

ei+1=zei en=zne0

That is a geometric sequence, and its whole character is the size of z. Above 1 it compounds: 1.05 per step looks harmless for a few steps and is a thousandfold after 140. Below 1 it decays toward zero. Exactly 1 holds steady. The comparison is with 1, not with 0; a ratio of 0.999 is still shrinking.

A ratio per step turns into a time by counting steps: the error doubles when zn=2, so after n=ln2/lnz steps, or h·ln2/lnz seconds. That is the doubling time on the spine.

Type a ratio and step it forward; then set it just above and just below 1 and watch how little the difference looks at first.
