Vectors

A vector is a length and a direction: an arrow. On the plane it is two numbers, its components (x,y), and its magnitude is the length of the arrow, x2+y2.

Adding two vectors is placing the tail of one at the tip of the other; the sum runs from the first tail to the last tip. The components add one at a time:

(x1,y1) +(x2,y2) =(x1+x2, y1+y2)

Multiplying a vector by a number stretches it, or flips it if the number is negative, and never turns it. That is all F=ma does to the force sum: a is the same arrow, scaled by 1/m.

These are the two arrows you just dragged on the spine, minus the mass. Drag the tip of either one and the sum, its components, and its magnitude update; drag one arrow around the other and watch the parallelogram close on the same sum either way.
