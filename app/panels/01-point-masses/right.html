Rigid bodies: extent and rotation

A rigid body is simply an object that has mass and dimensions that do not change. Step up from the point mass and the point grows into a shape. The mass spreads out over that shape, but it still has one centre — the centre of mass — and that centre is exactly where the point was.

Rigid bodies also have a rotation θ, measured in radians:

(x,y,θ) = ( 1.00, 0.50, 0.60 )

θ is just a coordinate like x and y. Drag any of the three numbers above sideways to scrub it, drag the body itself to move it, or drag the handle on its axis to spin it about the centre of mass.
