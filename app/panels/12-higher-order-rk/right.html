Explode order

The left pane cut the series at one λ, term by term. This is the same truncation across the whole plane, order by order: the regions for RK1 through RK4 overlaid, each the set of hλ where the degree-n Taylor polynomial of ehλ has modulus at most one:

|∑j=0n (hλ)jj! |≤1

Order 1 is the Euler disk. Order 2 is a slightly larger blob that still only touches the imaginary axis at the origin. Orders 3 and 4 are the first that reach across the axis and enclose a stretch of it, which is why RK4 survives the spring that broke Euler. The regions do not simply nest: RK4’s has lobes that RK3’s lacks, and every one of them stops a short way down the negative real axis. Shrinking the step size still scales every one of them toward the origin. And we can still see that there are issues with positive real values or extreme negative real values: no explicit order reaches the right half plane.

Hover an order to highlight its region and its polynomial and dim the rest.
