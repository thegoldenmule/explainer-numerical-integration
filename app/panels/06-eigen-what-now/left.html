Matrices as transforms

A 2×2 matrix is a rule for moving every point of the plane at once. It sends (x,y) to a new point whose coordinates are mixtures of the old ones:

[abcd] [xy] = [ax+bycx+dy]

The columns are where the two unit arrows land: the first column is the image of (1,0), the second of (0,1). Everything else follows, because the map is linear: grid lines stay straight and parallel, and the origin stays put.

Change the diagonal and the shape scales. Change the off-diagonal and it skews. Mix them as (cosθ,sinθ) columns and it rotates. No eigenvectors, no invariant directions yet: matrices as transformations, nothing more.

Drag the four entries and watch the shape scale, skew, and rotate.
