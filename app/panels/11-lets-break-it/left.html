The exact solution, no method

Drop the integrator. For one mode, the exact solution of x′=λx is x(t)=x0eλt, and with λ=α+iω that splits into a size and a turn:

eλt=eαt(cosωt+i·sinωt)

So the plane reads by example. On the negative real axis, ω=0: pure decay, a curve that slides to zero. On the imaginary axis, α=0: pure oscillation, forever, at the same amplitude. In between, a spiral in the plane and a ringing decay in time. In the right half plane the spiral runs outward and the real system itself is unstable; no method can fix that.

This is what a method is trying to reproduce. Every red curve in the piece is judged against a blue one drawn from this formula, and the disk on the spine is the set of λ where Euler’s version of it does not grow.

Pick a λ on the plane and see its closed-form curve; walk it from the real axis up to the imaginary one.
