Explode the plane: a grid of λ

A small grid of λ points, inside, on, and outside the boundary, each with a mini trajectory of the simulation against its exact curve. Hover a point — or one of the mini trajectories — to highlight it and dim the rest.

The left pane is one of these with no method; the spine is one of these with the current system; this is all of them, for whichever integration scheme you pick, the same choice as the spine’s.
