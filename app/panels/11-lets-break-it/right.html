Explode the plane: a grid of λ

The spine breaks one system. Here a small grid of λ points is placed inside, on, and outside the boundary, and each one carries a mini trajectory of the simulation against its exact curve.

Inside the disk the red curve tracks the blue and dies down with it. On the edge, the ratio is exactly one and the red curve holds its amplitude while the blue decays: stable by the letter, wrong in fact. Outside, using a method outside of its region of stability causes solutions to stray, slowly near the edge and violently far from it. The row along the imaginary axis is the one that matters for springs, and every point on it is out.

The left pane is one of these with no method; the spine is one of these with the current system; this is all of them. It is also the picture the shader draws, sampled: the shaded region is this grid taken to every pixel.

Hover a point to highlight its trajectory and dim the rest.
