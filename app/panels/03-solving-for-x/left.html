x, v, a: derivatives

Position x is where the mass is. Velocity v is how fast x is changing right now: the slope of the x curve at this instant. Acceleration a is how fast v is changing: the slope of the v curve.

v=x′=dxdt a=v′=x″=d2xdt2

That is why the force sum is an equation about x″: Newton hands us the slope of the slope, and we want x itself. Going from a back to v, and from v back to x, is integration, and doing it numerically is the whole of Part II.

This is one instant of the trajectory on the spine. Nudge t and watch the tangent tilt: the slope you see on x is the value of v, and the slope on v is the value of a.
