Assistant2026-09-02T17:42:28.383Z

It's in the admin, not on the kid screens. Tap the lock, enter the passcode, scroll to the "This device" section, and the button "Listen as a grown-up" sits under the two device toggles. Tapping it closes the admin and puts the band with "Back to kid mode" above the playing bar.

If the button isn't there, one of two things is true:

  • The device answers say the library is the kid's own. The button is only offered when the app thinks the library signed in is a grown-up's, which means either "Sharing this device with your child" is on, or "Your child has their own Apple Music account" is off. On a dedicated kid device the merged read is already on and grown-up mode is deliberately absent. Flip a toggle in that same section and the button appears.
  • Sync Library is off for the account, in which case the app hides it since there would be nothing to show.
